General Book Marketing

Do I really need to market my book?+

Amazon publishes over four million new books every year. Without a marketing strategy, your book will be invisible. Amazon's algorithm rewards books that generate early sales, so you need to drive those initial sales yourself.

How much should I budget for book marketing?+

Budget a minimum of $500 to $1,000 for a basic launch and $1,000 to $5,000+ for a comprehensive launch with ads, visual assets, Goodreads placement, and book club outreach. For ongoing ads, plan for $500 to $2,000 per month in ad spend plus management fees.

How long does it take to see results?+

Paid ads can generate sales within 1 to 2 weeks. Launch campaigns show dramatic results during launch week and the following 30 days. Platform building shows growth within 60 to 90 days. Goodreads placements drive traffic within 2 to 4 weeks. Book club outreach takes 4 to 6 weeks.

Is marketing different for fiction versus non-fiction?+

Yes, significantly. Fiction focuses on genre communities, tropes, and series read-through. Non-fiction focuses on authority building, credibility, and leveraging the book as a lead generation tool. I have experience with both.

Do I need a large online following?+

No. A small but engaged email list of 1,000 readers will outsell a disengaged audience of 50,000 every time. What matters is having a targeted marketing plan.

About My Services

Do you work with both self-published and traditionally published authors?+

Yes. I work with indie, hybrid, and traditionally published authors. Even with a publisher, you are responsible for most of your marketing.

Do you guarantee bestseller status?+

No. Be wary of anyone who does. I guarantee proven, data-driven strategies that give your book the best possible chance. I will always be transparent about realistic expectations.

Can I hire you for just one service?+

Absolutely. Many clients start with a single service like a Strategy Intensive or book mockup and expand later. Zero pressure to commit to more.

What is a book mockup?+

A photorealistic 3D rendering that makes your book look like a physical copy. It elevates your visual presentation, increases ad click-through rates, and gives reviewers compelling visuals. I have seen mockups alone double click-through rates.

What is Goodreads Listopia?+

Curated book lists on Goodreads that receive millions of views from readers actively looking for their next read. I get your book placed on the right lists for massive organic visibility.

What is Book Club Litopia outreach?+

A targeted campaign to get your book adopted by book clubs. I identify matching clubs, craft custom pitches, create discussion guides, and coordinate author Q and A sessions via email. A single adoption can drive dozens of bulk purchases.

Do you offer payment plans?+

Yes. I offer flexible payment options including monthly installments for most services.
