The Face Behind the Strategy

I Am Dorris Alexy, and I Turn Invisible Books Into Bestsellers

Book marketing strategist, lifelong reader, creative asset specialist, and the dedicated professional your book needs in its corner if you want to stop guessing and start selling.

My Story

How I Fell in Love With Book Marketing and Why I Will Never Do Anything Else

My love affair with books started long before I ever heard the word "marketing."

I was the kid who read under the covers with a flashlight long past bedtime. The teenager who spent every allowance at the bookstore instead of the mall. The college student who chose her elective classes based on which ones had the longest reading lists. Books were not just a hobby for me. They were my entire world and my greatest passion.

So when I started my professional career in marketing, it only made sense that I would eventually find my way to the publishing industry.

But the moment that changed everything for me, the moment that made me decide to dedicate my entire career to helping authors, happened when a close friend of mine self-published her very first novel.

She had spent three years writing it. She had poured her heart, her savings, and her soul into every single page. She hired a professional editor, invested in a gorgeous cover design, and formatted the interior perfectly. By every objective measure, it was a beautiful, well-crafted book that deserved to be read by thousands of people.

And when she hit "publish" on Amazon, almost nothing happened.

Day one: 12 sales, mostly from family members. Day two: 3 sales. Day three: zero.

She sent me an email in tears. "What did I do wrong?" she asked. "Is my book just not good enough?"

Her book was incredible. The problem was never the book itself. The problem was that she had absolutely no marketing plan in place.

That single email lit a fire inside me that has never gone out. I dove headfirst into everything I could learn about book marketing. I studied Amazon algorithms, ad platforms, email funnels, launch strategies, reader psychology, genre-specific tactics, Goodreads optimization, book club outreach, visual asset creation, and every other facet of the book marketing ecosystem.

Fast forward to today, and I have had the extraordinary privilege of working with 50+ authors across 15+ genres, helping them collectively generate millions in book sales and millions of reader impressions. I have produced hundreds of professional book mockups and trailers, secured placements on dozens of high-traffic Goodreads lists, and connected my clients' books with book clubs and reading groups across the country.

My Philosophy

What I Believe About Book Marketing

Marketing Is an Act of Service

Your book has the power to entertain, educate, inspire, or transform someone's life. Marketing is simply the process of connecting that book with the person who needs it most.

Data Over Gut Feelings

I make every decision based on real, verifiable data including click-through rates, conversion rates, cost per acquisition, reader demographics, and sales trends.

Long-Term Growth Over Quick Hacks

I build sustainable marketing systems that keep your book selling consistently month after month and year after year.

Visual Presentation Matters

Readers judge books by their covers, mockups, trailers, and every visual touchpoint. That is why I offer professional mockup design and cinematic trailer production.

Clear, Written Communication

I conduct all communication exclusively via email because I believe you deserve a permanent, searchable record of every strategy and decision we make together.
