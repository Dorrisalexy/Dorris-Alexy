Contemporary Romance Book Launch Campaign

The Debut Romance Author Who Hit Number One

Client: Author M. | Genre: Contemporary Romance

The Situation

Author M. was a first-time self-published author with a completed manuscript, a professionally designed cover, and absolutely zero marketing experience. She had no email list, no online following, and her book was scheduled to publish in just 10 weeks. She emailed me in a state of panic, convinced she had already missed her window.

The Strategy

We built a compressed 10-week launch plan via email. I recruited an ARC team of 120 readers, optimized her Amazon listing, grew her email list from zero to 1,800 subscribers in 8 weeks, created professional 3D book mockups, and crafted a launch week email sequence alongside targeted Amazon Ads.

The Results

  • Number one New Release in Contemporary Romance on Amazon
  • Number one in three additional sub-categories
  • Over 250 verified reviews within 30 days
  • More than 4,200 copies sold in the first month
  • Email list grew to over 3,500 subscribers within 60 days
  • Remained in Amazon Top 100 for 8 consecutive weeks
"Dorris took my book from completely invisible to a category bestseller in under two weeks. Her launch strategy was flawless, her communication was incredibly clear, and she handled every single detail."
Author M., Contemporary Romance
Business and Self-Help Ads Management

The Non-Fiction Author Who Tripled His Sales in 45 Days

Client: Author R.K. | Genre: Business and Self-Help

The Situation

Author R.K. had published his book six months prior and was selling 15 to 20 copies per week. He was spending $800 per month on Amazon ads with a return of only 0.6x, losing money on every click. He emailed me ready to abandon advertising entirely.

The Strategy

I audited his ad account and found overly broad keywords, no negative keyword list, and a poorly optimized listing. I restructured everything, rewrote his ad copy, created professional mockups for his creatives, and set up Meta retargeting campaigns.

The Results

  • Return on ad spend improved from 0.6x to 3.8x within 45 days
  • Ad spend decreased by 30% while sales increased by 220%
  • Weekly sales grew from 15 to 20 copies to 60 to 80 copies
  • Book re-entered the Amazon Top 50 in its category
  • Author began receiving speaking invitations from increased visibility
"I was about to give up on advertising entirely. Dorris identified problems I had no idea existed and restructured everything within two weeks. The results were almost immediate."
Author R.K., Business and Self-Help

The Fantasy Series Author Who Built a Sustainable Platform

Client: Author T.J. | Genre: Epic Fantasy

The Situation

Author T.J. had published four books in her fantasy series over three years but never invested in marketing. Sales were declining with each release, her email list had fewer than 200 subscribers, and her online presence was nonexistent.

The Strategy

We executed a 6-month platform growth strategy via email. I helped create a reader magnet, designed an email funnel, developed a content strategy, produced a cinematic book trailer, secured Goodreads Listopia placements, and connected her with fantasy book clubs.

The Results

  • Email list grew from under 200 to over 8,500 subscribers in 6 months
  • Backlist sales increased by 350% across all four books
  • Book five launch sold over 5,000 copies in the first week
  • Goodreads placement drove 15,000+ organic impressions per month
  • Three book clubs adopted the series for quarterly reading lists
  • Author now generates consistent monthly income from her backlist
"She built me an entire marketing infrastructure from scratch. The book trailer and Goodreads placements alone were game changers. She changed my entire career."
Author T.J., Epic Fantasy

The Thriller Author Who Revived a Dead Backlist

Client: Author S.L. | Genre: Psychological Thriller

The Situation

Author S.L. had three published thriller novels that had been out for over two years and were collectively selling fewer than 30 copies per month. The books had good reviews but virtually no visibility on Amazon.

The Strategy

I rewrote all three book descriptions, repositioned each book into strategic niche categories, implemented a series-wide Amazon Ads campaign, created professional 3D mockups, produced a suspenseful book trailer for video ads, secured Goodreads Listopia placements, and reached out to thriller book clubs.

The Results

  • Combined monthly sales increased from 30 to over 400 copies within 60 days
  • Book one achieved a new all-time sales rank on Amazon
  • Read-through rate from book one to book two increased by 180%
  • Ad campaigns achieved a consistent 4.2x return on ad spend
  • Book trailer video ads outperformed static ads by 65%
  • Goodreads placement generated over 8,000 organic impressions monthly
  • Two book clubs adopted the first book for their reading selection
  • Monthly royalty income increased by over 1,200%
"I honestly thought my backlist was beyond saving. Dorris proved me wrong in the first month. The mockups, trailer, and Goodreads placements gave my books a completely new life."
Author S.L., Psychological Thriller
