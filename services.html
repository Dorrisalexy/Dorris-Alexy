Service Number 1

Full Book Launch Campaign

Launch your book like a bestseller, not an afterthought. A complete, done-for-you 90-day book launch campaign that builds pre-release buzz, dominates launch week, and keeps your book selling long after the initial excitement fades.

Why Launch Strategy Matters

Amazon's algorithm pays extremely close attention to what happens in the first 30 days after your book is published. If your book generates a strong surge of sales, downloads, and reviews during that critical window, Amazon begins recommending your book to more readers organically. You get placed in "Customers Also Bought" carousels, you show up higher in search results, and you get featured in genre-specific email recommendations sent to millions of readers.

But if your book launches to silence, the algorithm buries it. And once your book is buried, it is incredibly difficult and expensive to dig it back out. That is why your launch strategy is the single most important marketing investment you will ever make.

Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Days 1 through 60 Before Publication)

  • Market and competitor research analyzing the top 20 bestselling books in your genre
  • Amazon listing optimization including title, subtitle, description, and backend keywords
  • Category strategy identifying the best 3 to 5 Amazon categories for maximum ranking
  • ARC team recruitment and management of 50 to 200+ advance readers
  • Pre-launch buzz campaign with countdown strategy, cover reveal, and teaser content
  • Email list warm-up sequence of 5 professionally written emails
  • Professional book mockup creation for all promotional materials

Phase 2: Launch Week (Days 1 through 7 After Publication)

  • Complete launch day coordination of all moving pieces
  • 7-day launch week email sequence designed for maximum sales velocity
  • Strategic Amazon and Meta ad campaign activation
  • Real-time monitoring and troubleshooting of sales, rankings, and reviews
  • Social proof amplification using early reviews and milestones

Phase 3: Post-Launch Momentum (Days 8 through 30)

  • Post-launch ad optimization with daily monitoring
  • Continued review generation strategy
  • Price promotions, newsletter swaps, Goodreads placement, and book club outreach
  • Comprehensive 30-day performance report delivered via email

This Service Is Ideal If:

  • Your book publishes within the next 3 to 6 months
  • You are a debut author wanting a strong first impression
  • You have launched before but were disappointed
  • You want a hands-off, done-for-you experience
  • You are willing to invest in long-term success

This Might Not Be the Best Fit If:

  • Your book has been out for more than 6 months
  • You have a very limited budget
  • You prefer to execute everything yourself

Service Number 2

Book Ads Management

Stop wasting money on book ads that do not work. I create, manage, and optimize high-converting ad campaigns across Amazon, Facebook, Instagram, and BookBub that drive real book sales and a profitable return on your advertising investment.

Why Most Authors Lose Money on Ads

Ads absolutely work for books. Some of the most commercially successful authors generate the majority of their income through paid advertising. But there is a massive difference between throwing money at an ad platform and running a strategic, professionally optimized campaign.

The most common mistakes include targeting the wrong audience with generic keywords, using weak ad creative with flat cover images instead of professional 3D mockups, failing to test systematically, ignoring the conversion funnel by sending traffic to poorly optimized Amazon listings, and giving up too soon before campaigns have gathered enough data.

Month 1: Audit, Research, and Setup

  • Complete audit of any existing ad accounts across Amazon, Meta, and BookBub
  • Deep-dive keyword research specific to your genre and sub-genre
  • Competitor ad analysis to understand top-performing strategies
  • Detailed audience profiling and targeting strategy
  • Campaign architecture design including structure, ad groups, and bidding
  • Professional ad copywriting including headlines, descriptions, and hooks
  • Ad creative direction with professional book mockups for higher click-through rates
  • Full campaign setup and launch across all agreed platforms
  • Amazon listing audit and optimization recommendations

Month 2: Testing and Optimization

  • Systematic A/B testing of ad copy, creative assets, and audiences
  • Daily monitoring of all campaign performance metrics
  • Strategic bid adjustments and budget reallocation based on data
  • Negative keyword identification and implementation
  • Click-through rate and conversion rate optimization
  • Weekly performance updates delivered to your email inbox

Month 3 and Beyond: Scaling

  • Scaling winning campaigns by increasing budgets on high-performing ads
  • Pausing or restructuring underperforming campaigns
  • Expanding into new platforms, audiences, or keyword categories
  • Seasonal and promotional campaign planning
  • Integration of book trailer video ads for higher engagement
  • Comprehensive monthly performance reports via email
  • Ongoing strategy emails to discuss results and next steps

Platforms I Manage

Amazon Ads: Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, and Lockscreen Ads. The number one platform for driving direct book sales.

Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram): Ideal for building awareness, growing your email list, and targeting readers. Video ads with book trailers perform exceptionally well.

BookBub Ads: A highly targeted platform reaching avid readers who buy discounted books. Excellent for price promotions and backlist sales.

Goodreads Listopia Placement

Get your book discovered by millions of readers on the world's largest book community. Strategic placement on popular, high-traffic Goodreads lists where avid readers actively browse for their next read.

Why Goodreads Listopia Is Massively Underutilized

Goodreads has over 90 million members actively browsing the platform for their next read. Goodreads Listopia is a feature where users create and vote on curated book lists organized by genre, theme, mood, or topic. Examples include lists like "Best Psychological Thrillers of All Time" or "Must-Read Romance Novels of the Decade."

These lists receive millions of views from readers who are in active discovery mode and ready to buy. When your book appears on the right lists, it gains massive organic visibility that translates directly into Amazon sales without spending a single dollar on advertising. Despite this enormous potential, the vast majority of authors completely overlook Goodreads as a marketing channel.

Book Club Litopia Outreach

Get your book adopted by book clubs across the country. Targeted outreach to book clubs, reading groups, and literary communities to create a powerful word-of-mouth sales engine for your book.

Why Book Clubs Are Incredibly Powerful

Book clubs represent one of the most powerful and consistently underutilized sales channels in publishing. A single book club adoption can result in dozens of bulk purchases because every member buys or receives a copy. But the impact goes far beyond the initial purchase.

When a book club reads and discusses your book, every member becomes a potential advocate. They recommend it to friends, family, and colleagues. They leave reviews on Amazon and Goodreads. They post about it online. The word-of-mouth effect ripples outward for months, creating organic sales momentum that no paid advertisement can replicate.

Most authors have no idea how to identify the right book clubs, craft a pitch that resonates, or provide supporting materials that make adoption easy. That is exactly what this service solves.

What Is Included

  • Extensive research to identify book clubs matching your genre, themes, and audience
  • Custom pitch letter development highlighting why your book is perfect for discussion
  • Targeted outreach campaign to book clubs with personalized pitches
  • Professional discussion guide creation with thoughtful questions and conversation starters
  • Author Q and A coordination via email for clubs wanting direct engagement
  • Detailed outreach report tracking pitches sent, responses received, and adoptions confirmed
  • Follow-up strategy to maintain relationships for future book releases
