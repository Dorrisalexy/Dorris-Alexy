Book Launches | 8 Minute Read

The 7 Biggest Book Launch Mistakes Authors Make and How to Avoid Every Single One

Launching a book is one of the most exciting and simultaneously most stressful experiences an author can go through. But here is the uncomfortable reality: a great book paired with a poor launch strategy will almost always underperform commercially.

Mistake Number 1: Starting Your Launch Planning Far Too Late

A successful book launch requires a minimum of 90 days of active preparation, and ideally six months. If your book has not been published yet, start planning now.

Mistake Number 2: Skipping the ARC Review Phase

You need a minimum of 20 to 30 reviews on your Amazon page by launch day. That means you need an ARC team reading your book four to eight weeks before publication.

Mistake Number 3: Publishing Without Optimizing Your Amazon Listing

If your blurb is weak, your keywords are incorrect, or your categories are too broad, readers will not convert into buyers no matter how much traffic you drive.

Mistake Number 4: Relying Exclusively on Friends and Family

Friends and family purchases do not trigger the Amazon algorithm meaningfully. You need to reach actual readers in your genre.

Mistake Number 5: Completely Ignoring Email Marketing

Email is the single most effective marketing channel for authors. Your email list is the only audience you truly own and control.

Mistake Number 6: Setting Up Ads and Walking Away

Ads require daily monitoring, bid adjustments, negative keyword management, and continuous creative testing.

Mistake Number 7: Stopping All Marketing After Launch Week

The most successful authors continue marketing aggressively for at least 30 to 90 days after publication. Do not let your book fade on day eight.

Conclusion: Avoiding these seven mistakes will put you miles ahead of the majority of authors who launch without a plan.
